1. Purpose and vision

The Relief Management System supports a humanitarian non-profit (modelled on the Gift of the Givers Foundation) in three areas:

  1. Raising money transparently. The public can donate online. Every rand shown as "raised" can be traced to a confirmed payment or a staff-recorded offline contribution.
  2. Mobilising people. Volunteers apply online. Staff review them and assign them to relief projects.
  3. Accountability to donors. Donors see their history and download donation certificates for confirmed gifts.

Product principle — honesty over polish. The system never shows a figure, status or document it cannot back with data. Unpaid donations are labelled as such, demonstration content is labelled as demonstration, and statistics are computed from the database.

Context

The project is an academic deliverable (Portfolio of Evidence, "Section B" ERD). It is hosted publicly as a demonstration and uses a real NGO's name for illustration. It is not affiliated with that organisation. Payments use the PayFast sandbox until real merchant details are configured.

3. Scope

In scope (current release)

  • Public information pages, live impact figures, project listing with funding progress.
  • Online donations in ZAR, USD and EUR, paid through PayFast in rand.
  • Donation receipts, donor dashboard and history, donation certificates (HTML + PDF).
  • Volunteer applications, review, approval/rejection and assignment to projects.
  • Contact inquiries and their handling.
  • Staff management of projects, project updates, offline funds and funding reconciliation.
  • Staff access management and an audit log.
  • Privacy notice and consent capture.

Out of scope (this release)

  • Automatic recurring billing. Monthly pledges are recorded, but each payment is made separately.
  • Live exchange rates (rates are configured).
  • Native mobile apps (the site is responsive).
  • Multi-organisation or multi-tenant use, and multiple languages.
  • Accounting and general-ledger integration.

5. Business rules

IDRule
BR-01A project's raised figure = offline funds (staff-recorded) + rand value of its Verified donations. Nothing else changes it.
BR-02A donation is Pending from submission until a validated PayFast notification marks it Verified (COMPLETE) or Failed (any other status).
BR-03PayFast is charged the rand amount: AmountZar = round(Amount × rate, 2), where the rate is stored with the donation.
BR-04A certificate exists only for a Verified donation. It is visible to its owner and to staff. Anonymous donations show "Anonymous Donor".
BR-05Certificates are labelled demonstration until Organisation:PboReference is set and Organisation:IsDemo is false.
BR-06Public registration never grants staff access.
BR-07Every staff change to data or access is written to the audit log in the same transaction.
BR-08Personal data is collected only with recorded consent (timestamp per record).
BR-09Single-donation limit: 1 to 1,000,000 in the chosen currency.
